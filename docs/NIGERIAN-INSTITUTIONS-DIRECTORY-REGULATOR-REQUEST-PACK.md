# Regulator Data Access and Reuse Request Pack

**Prepared for:** BrainLab Academy  
**Purpose:** Request official structured institution and programme data, permitted reuse terms, update cadence and correction procedures.  
**Status:** Draft for review and sending by BrainLab; no request has been sent by this document.

## 1. Common request

BrainLab Academy is developing a Nigerian Institutions and Programmes Directory to help learners select and maintain accurate academic institution and programme information. We intend to display regulator-attributed factual directory information, distinguish institution recognition from programme-specific approval/accreditation, and show the date and source of each verification.

We respectfully request:

1. The latest official institution register in CSV, XLSX, JSON or another machine-readable format, including stable regulator identifiers where available.
2. The latest programme catalogue and institution-specific programme/offering records, including award/qualification, campus/location, approval/accreditation status, effective date, expiry/end date and any restrictions.
3. The source's definitions for institution types, ownership, programme levels and every approval/accreditation status.
4. Written permission and conditions for BrainLab Academy to extract, store, normalise, attribute, display and periodically refresh the relevant factual directory fields on its website and student-facing application.
5. Any required attribution wording, logo/branding restrictions, disclaimer, licensing fee or other conditions.
6. The recommended update frequency, official change-notification channel and process for reporting corrections.
7. Confirmation of which data fields may not be republished and whether historical snapshots may be retained for audit and student academic-history purposes.

We will retain source names, source dates and regulator identifiers; avoid implying that a historical snapshot is a current approval; provide a correction channel; and not reproduce full publications or protected explanatory material unless separately authorised.

## 2. NBTE — specific request

**To:** National Board for Technical Education (NBTE)  
**Suggested email:** enquiries@nbte.gov.ng  
**Official register:** https://digitalnbte.nbte.gov.ng/Public  
**2025 directory landing page:** https://web.nbte.gov.ng/node/114

In addition to the common request, please provide:

- Current institution register export with institution category, ownership, state/LGA, official name, status and NBTE identifier.
- Current institution-specific programme accreditation export, including exact programme name, award level, accreditation status, approved streams, accreditation effective date and accreditation end/expiry date.
- The current programme/trade catalogue and mapping from institution-specific records to canonical programme identifiers.
- Clarification of how the 25th Edition 2025 Directory should be interpreted alongside the current online register, including the difference between the printed 2025 summary and current live-register totals.
- Written permission to extract and republish permitted factual institution/programme/accreditation fields from the 25th Edition 2025 Directory in BrainLab's directory, subject to NBTE's conditions.
- Confirmation whether NBTE can provide a current structured replacement for the PDF and a correction/update feed.

The supplied 25th Edition 2025 PDF contains a copyright notice reserving reproduction rights and requiring prior written permission for reproduction, except brief academic quotations. BrainLab therefore seeks express written permission before bulk republication of any extracted records.

## 3. NUC — specific request

**To:** National Universities Commission (NUC)  
**Suggested email:** info@nuc.edu.ng  
**Official Nigerian University System:** https://enuc.nuc.edu.ng/nus

Please provide the latest university register and university-specific approved programme data in machine-readable form, including institution identifier, official name, ownership, state, establishment/licensing status, campus details where available, programme title, award, approval/accreditation status, effective dates and any relevant restrictions. Please also confirm the permitted attribution and reuse terms for factual fields displayed by BrainLab, the update cadence, and the process for reporting corrections or newly licensed/renamed/closed institutions.

## 4. NCCE — specific request

**To:** National Commission for Colleges of Education (NCCE)  
**Suggested email:** info@ncce.gov.ng  
**Official Accredited Colleges list:** https://www.ncce.gov.ng/AccreditedColleges

Please provide the current accredited colleges/NCE-awarding institutions register in machine-readable form, including official institution name, underlying institution type, ownership, state, location, NCE-awarding status and current accreditation/approval details for NCE programmes. Please identify polytechnics and other institutions authorised to award NCE separately from Colleges of Education. Please confirm reuse/attribution terms, update cadence and the correction process.

## 6. Sending and follow-up checklist

- [ ] Confirm the sender's official BrainLab Academy contact details.
- [ ] Send the relevant regulator-specific request to the verified official address.
- [ ] Retain the sent email and all responses in the source evidence register.
- [ ] Record permission scope, permitted fields, attribution, restrictions, effective date and expiry/review date.
- [ ] Do not publish bulk extracted NBTE 2025 PDF records until written permission is received and reviewed.
- [ ] If no structured export is available, ask for an authorised alternative process; do not bypass authentication, CAPTCHA, rate limits or undocumented interfaces.
- [ ] Record the regulator's nominated contact and agreed refresh schedule.
